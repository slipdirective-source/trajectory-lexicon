# v1.4 proposed amendment: T_unk decision-dominance (narrow scope)

**Status: PROPOSED.** Proposer: Microsoft 365 Copilot (relayed 2026-10-18). Class: Doctrine. Requires Caleb Meadows' explicit word before cut. Silence is not approval.

## Amendment

In §13 (Safety states, triggers, and admissibility), replace the `T_unk` trigger row:

**From:** Unresolved exposure becomes decision-dominant.

**To:** Unresolved exposure is decision-dominant iff at least one admissible resolution of that uncertainty changes the current admissibility outcome. Unresolved exposure remains represented whether or not it is decision-dominant. Non-decision-dominant uncertainty does not independently block execution.

## Rationale

Battery case A5b demonstrated that dependency alone — `Depends(I,U)` — is too coarse to govern blocking: a material U whose admissible resolutions all leave the admissibility outcome unchanged should not block. The governing property is **counterfactual sensitivity of the admissibility decision to admissible resolutions of U**. `Depends` remains necessary *information* (recorded, e.g. in interface `unresolvedDependencies`) but is not the blocking discriminator and is not promoted into the core as one.

## Conformance with existing structure

- Preserves the standing distinction: trajectory envelopes retain unresolved exposure (invariant 7; §6 mandatory U branch in `Gset`), while `T_unk` concerns only unresolved exposure *becoming decision-dominant*.
- Gives battery cases A5a/A5b/A5c a direct specification target; they become normative against this definition when v1.4 is cut.
